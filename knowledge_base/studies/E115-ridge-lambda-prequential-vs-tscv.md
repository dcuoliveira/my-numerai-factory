## Main question
Does choosing ridge's λ by prequential loss (argmin mean per-period loss over all resolved out-of-sample forecasts) instead of in-window time-series CV change its score — and does selecting on payout itself raise payout?

## What was tried
One fixed grid of 51 log-spaced alphas (1e-3..1e7, sklearn scale, bracketing the X'X spectrum ~0.024..2e4). At every refit the ridge is fitted for every alpha (one SVD) and two rules pick the column: PREQUENTIAL (argmin of each alpha's mean per-period loss over its own resolved out-of-sample forecasts; 46 warm-up blocks from 2020-08 give history) vs TSCV (argmin mean held-out loss over 5 purged folds inside the training window — the pipeline's own rule; tscv/corr reproduces hyper_params_search). Losses: −numerai_corr, mse, −payout (vs the real meta model, which starts 2024-05-07, so early payout picks stand on few periods). 6 arms from one shared pass. Paired preq−tscv per loss and loss-vs-loss per rule (Newey-West lag 20) on 510 common periods, with an ex-post fixed-λ CORR and payout curve. The two payout arms were added 2026-10-23; rerunning reproduced the four earlier arms byte-for-byte.

## What was found
FINDINGS-BACKED. The rule does not matter: preq − tscv is null under all three losses (payout 95% CIs [−0.0132,+0.0071] corr, [−0.0054,+0.0062] mse, [−0.0051,+0.0067] payout). λ does matter and both rules find it: the ex-post fixed-λ CORR curve runs 0.1046 (least squares) up to 0.1373 at λ≈3,981 then down to 0.1234; every arm sits within 0.0017 CORR of the peak (selection recovers ~95% of the 0.033 gap). They get there differently — prequential locks onto λ≈2,512 (MSE arm never moves; −CORR drops to 1,585 for 3 blocks), TSCV re-decides every month (14 distinct λ, 20 switches in 24 blocks). Arm scores (payout): prequential −CORR 0.06532, prequential MSE 0.06627, TSCV −CORR 0.06837, TSCV MSE 0.06591, prequential −payout 0.06636, TSCV −payout 0.06556. TSCV/−CORR has the best risk profile (calmar 0.045 vs 0.038, dd 1.52 vs 1.74) but not significantly (payout t +0.59 vs prequential). Choosing on payout does not raise payout: null under prequential (+0.0010, t +0.21, but lowest dd(pay) 1.43); HURTS under TSCV (−0.0028 payout t −2.09, −0.0061 CORR t −2.58) — the one comparison of 15 clearing |t|>=2, read as a warning. The meta model's short history makes payout a noisy utility (17 evidence periods at block 1 vs 992 for CORR). Context: every arm trails 6-monthly e109_ridge_fenc by 0.015–0.016 CORR, a cadence effect (the pipeline's own monthly e61_glm_reg_fenccausal scores 0.13644; the 6-monthly twin 0.14357). Verdict: λ-selection rule is not a lever; refit cadence is the bigger one (→ E116). Scouts ran at era_step 4, not reportable.
