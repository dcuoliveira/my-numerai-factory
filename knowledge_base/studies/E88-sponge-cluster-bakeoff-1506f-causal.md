## Main question
The E87 idea on the bigger causal-combo: which model best exploits which signed cluster of the 1506f stack, and does a cross-cluster/model blend beat the full combo or the deployed book? No single cluster is expected to beat the full combo (which trails raw).

## What was tried
25 SPONGE signed-spectral clusters (k=25 by silhouette) partitioning all 1506 features exactly (sizes 330/308/198/165/119/66/48/46/45/44/23/22/22/22/16/8/8/4/3/3/2/1/1/1/1), each selected via a `--feature-include` anchored alternation. **7 of the 25 are excluded** from the fitted roster — the pure day-level break-even-inflation clusters (sponge_02/03/06/14/19/21/24, 12 `feature_macro_bei_*` feats) are constant within each date, so within-date numerai_corr is undefined; the runner detects this data-drivenly (within-date nunique on a ~30-date sample) and names the exclusion, matching the E80/E81 BEI-inert prior. Leaves 18 active clusters × 5 models = 90 from-scratch runs at the shared protocol, each paired (NW lag 20) vs the full 1506f combo (`e85_*_fenc`); ensemble screen over the 90 cells + book. Clusters concurrent (CLUSTER_JOBS=5), 21 h, 0 failures.

## What was found
FINDINGS-BACKED (EXPERIMENTS.md). **(1) The signal concentrates in the volatility cluster.** sponge_09 (48 f) reproduces the full 1506 f combo across every model — paired NW vs `e85_<m>_fenc`: ridge payout +0.0136 (t +0.65; CORR +0.040 t +1.76), lgb +0.0057 (t +0.53), catboost +0.0006 (t +0.06), rf −0.0026 (t −0.32), mlp +0.0164 (t +0.90; CORR +0.046 t +2.32). The 16 f volume/price cluster sponge_07 nearly matches; the ~1300 interaction + `_symmean` features add nothing standalone and dilute ridge/mlp. **(2) Model × cluster:** rf/lgb/catboost extract the big signed-interaction clusters (sponge_05 330 f, sponge_01 308 f) where ridge & mlp collapse to ≈0; on the compact vol/volume/price clusters all five agree; pure-ix clusters (16/20/17) ≈ null. **(3) No single cell beats the book:** best cell rf/sponge_07 payout 0.0709 (446-period common window) vs SACI_06 0.0917 and CU_SA_IA_BO_01 0.107 — a feature-attribution result, not a new slot. **(4) A cluster-blend beats the kitchen sink:** a prediction-level rank-average of 5 rf cluster-cells (sponge_07+09+05+01+11) pays **0.0804** (own 521-window) = **+0.0139 vs the full combo `e85_rf_ktz_fenc` (t +2.09), MMC +0.0126 (t +2.13)**; on the 446 book-window it reaches 0.0895 ≈ SACI_06 (dpay −0.002, t −0.17). Notably the payout-series screen MISSED this (0/7140 blends beat their best member at |t|>2) — the blend's gain is in MMC, which the series-average screen cannot see. Board: results/reports/crypto/e88_sponge_board.html; blends priced as rf_ktz/e88_rf_blend5 etc. From the metrics, the top screened ensemble file e88_subens4_best shows payout 0.11080 / CORR 0.17413 / MMC 0.09339 on its 446-period window.
