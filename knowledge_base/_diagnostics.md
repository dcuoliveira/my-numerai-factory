## Main question
Can a from-scratch / unregularised torch MLP on the 44-column `fenccausal` panel beat or tie a same-window ridge over a 12-fold walk-forward, and under which training objective (MSE, −CORR per-day Pearson, or −MMC)? And, once a network's edge is found, does moving its predictions off the lagged Numerai meta model (residualisation) convert that edge into payable MMC/payout? The diagnostic panels exist to read these at the procedure level (K refit trajectories), because a metric curve alone is not a diagnosis.

## What was tried
A diagnostic campaign spanning several linked studies, each tag = one walk-forward refit saved with full per-epoch snapshots:

- **`torchscratch_corr_500ep_wf` (26 tags, 2 seeds × ~12 refits):** the from-scratch identity `(64,32,16)` net, alpha 0, 3-day batches, **trained on minus the per-day Pearson CORR**, 500 epochs, `--no-stop`. The headline arm.
- **`torchscratch_mse_500ep_wf` (26 tags):** same net and schedule, **MSE loss** — isolates "is the collapse the loss or the recipe?"
- **`torchscratch_mmc_meta_500ep_wf` (25 tags):** the **−MMC loss on the 547 meta days only**, cut into 12 folds of ~33 days (`--meta-only --scheme even`) — tests whether optimising MMC directly beats the CORR loss.
- **`torchmlp_noreg_500ep_wf` (25 tags) and `torchmlp_default_noreg_500ep_s42_full` (12 tags):** the **default relu `(64,32)` MLP, alpha 0**, 500 epochs, no early stop (the older `_full` set had 4 folds killed at 5–15 epochs; the `_wf` set is the clean re-run, 2 seeds).
- **`ref_e105_onecycle` / `ref_e105_const` (2+2 tags):** E105 scheduler reference panels on the degenerate **alpha 100** identity arm (OneCycleLR vs constant 1e-3), 120 epochs — ground the E105 finding.
- **`ref_torchmlp_relu_a001` (1 tag):** the relu **alpha 0.01** `torch_mlp` reference panel (the overfitting-from-epoch-5 case in the training-diagnostics standard).

Shared protocol across the big groups: `crypto/v2.0-fenccausal` (44f), `target_binned_return_20`, rolling tw130/refit130/embargo20, `val_frac=0.3`, seeds 42 and 43, GPU. The procedure-level read-out (P1–P6: per-fold overfitting table, best-epoch vs stop-rule epoch, gap to a same-window ridge, seed churn, refit churn, and CORR-gap-vs-MMC-gap) comes from `walkforward_diagnostics.py`.

Note: the related **E104** (day- vs row-minibatches) and **E105** (LR schedulers) studies were run on the degenerate **alpha 100** arm and are summarised in their own EXPERIMENTS.md sections; only their small reference panels live in this diag tree. E106/E107 (meta-residualisation) involve **no training** and read the stitched epoch-500 predictions that these walk-forward panels produce.

## What was found
The campaign's recorded conclusions, by objective (primary source `walkforward-noreg-mlp.md`; MMC-mechanism numbers corroborated in EXPERIMENTS.md §E106):

- **−CORR loss (`torchscratch_corr`) — TIES/beats the ridge on CORR, but not on payout/MMC.** It beats the same-window ridge on CORR in **11/12 folds, +0.0036 (t +2.9) over 1,531 days**, but the payout gain is not significant (**t +1.0**). Train CORR is only ~0.19, so it never overfits — it behaves as a linear model; seed churn 0.005, refit churn 0.47 (equal to the ridge's), and its per-target-bin profile matches the ridge's.
- **MSE loss (`torchscratch_mse`) — ties, and clears up the earlier "collapse".** CORR 0.1134; it **ties the −CORR network (t +1.1) and the ridge (t −1.6)**, and beats the relu-MSE recipe by +0.067 (relu t −8.0). So the first report's collapse was the **relu `torch_mlp` recipe, not the MSE loss**. Under MSE the ranking settles only ~epoch 190 while the loss is flat by ~epoch 5, so the stop rule fires too early (~epoch 22), which would cost ~0.014 CORR. Net map shrinks 1.5 → 0.14 under MSE; under −CORR it grows to 54.
- **−MMC loss on meta days (`torchscratch_mmc_meta`) — overfits and loses to the ridge.** Train MMC 0.16 but held-out MMC peaks by epoch 3 in 6/12 folds; at epoch 500 **MMC 0.023 vs 0.033 for the same-fold ridge (dpay t −2.3)**. The stop rule only brings it level. A layout confound: on meta-only folds the ridge's own MMC halves (0.033 vs 0.069 on full-layout folds), so the −CORR network's apparent 3× MMC there is mostly layout, not the loss; a clean −CORR-on-meta-folds comparison was never run.
- **No-reg relu MLP (`torchmlp_noreg` / `torchmlp_default_noreg_full`) — overfits and loses to the ridge 12/12.** Every fold is in the overfitting row; best-epoch median 19 while the stop rule fires at 84–116 (always too late); gap to a same-window ridge at epoch 500 is **median −0.057, below the ridge in 12/12 folds**; refit-boundary churn 0.89 vs a 0.67 seed floor and the ridge's 0.46. Its deficit is weak separation of the bottom target bin (the top-bin dip appears in the ridge too, so it is the target, not the net).
- **Why the CORR edge does not reach MMC (2026-10-19 rebuild; EXPERIMENTS.md §E106 "Why it works", FINDINGS-BACKED).** On the 547 meta days the networks rank coins almost exactly like the same-window ridge (per-day correlation 0.97); their departure from it **leans toward the meta model (correlation +0.09)**, and MMC does not pay for that part. Daily, **MMC gap ≈ −0.0024 + 0.71 × CORR gap**, so the −CORR network's CORR edge (+0.0054, t +2.5) is only +0.0014 on MMC; both networks beat the ridge on CORR in 4–5 of 5 covered folds but on MMC in only 2 (r09, r11).
- **What the edge was converted into (E106/E107, no training, FINDINGS-BACKED).** Residualising the −CORR network on a ≥5-day-old meta model, `rank(gauss(rank(p)) − s·β·gauss(rank(m)))`, raises payout **+0.0069 / +0.0064 (t +2.9 / +2.8)** for seeds 42/43 — the transform removes exactly the meta-leaning part the CORR edge was made of. It is dead by a 30-day-old meta model and costs drawdown above s≈0.5. Applied to all 38 models (E107, s=0.5) every model gains +0.003 to +0.010 (all t ≥ 2.2) but they do **not** converge (rank ρ 0.98), so model choice is not settled by it.
- **E105 reference panels (`ref_e105_*`, FINDINGS-BACKED §E105).** On the alpha-100 degenerate arm, annealing the LR to ~0 rescues it: OneCycleLR payout +0.0356 (t +9.2), CORR≥0.10 seeds 35→69, constant predictors 20→0 — early stopping in LR-time, not a better fit.

**Bottom line for deletion:** the from-scratch networks only **tie** the ridge (CORR edge under the −CORR loss, not a payout/MMC edge), the no-reg relu MLP **loses 12/12**, and the value that was banked (meta-residualisation) is captured in E106/E107 forecasts + `src.prediction.meta_resid`, not in these panels. The panels are per-epoch diagnostic arrays, re-derivable by re-fitting on GPU; the scientific conclusions above are already written into EXPERIMENTS.md and the memory files.
